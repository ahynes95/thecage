How it goes down

ONE COURT. ONE WINNER.

Show up to The Cage in San Antonio on October 10th-11th. Toss $10 in the pot, throw your name on the board, and get drafted onto a squad. It's 4-on-4, single location, all day. Last team standing walks away with a Dick's Sporting Goods gift card. No egos, no travel, just hoops.

📍

Single Location
